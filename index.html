    05 — the fine print

    What I draw & terms

    ✓ Happy to draw

    • Portraits from your photos
    • Original characters (OC)
    • Fanart of games, series, anime
    • Couples & friends
    • Pets & little creatures in the scene

    ✕ Won’t draw

    • NSFW / explicit content
    • Hate, harassment, real-life gore
    • Complex mecha & heavy architecture
    • Hyperrealism or copying another artist’s style
    • Anything for AI training or NFTs

    Payment

    50% upfront, 50% before the final file. PayPal or bank card — details in DM.

    Revisions

    2 free rounds at the sketch stage. Big changes after colouring may cost extra.

    Delivery

    High-res PNG (3000px+ on the long side), sent by email, Telegram or Google Drive.

    Timing

    Usually 1–3 weeks depending on the queue. Rush orders under 7 days are possible.

    Usage

    Personal use: avatars, posts with credit, prints for yourself. Commercial use only by agreement.

    Refunds

    Full refund before the sketch, 50% after the sketch, none once colouring has started.

    06 — questions

    Good to know

    Didn’t find your answer? Message me ↗

    Can you draw me from a photo?

    Yes! Send one or a few clear photos with good lighting. I’ll keep your features recognisable while painting them in my style.

    What should I send with my request?

    References of the person or character, the pose or mood you imagine, favourite colours and any must-have details (piercings, tattoos, accessories, pets).

    Can I print the artwork?

    Of course — for personal use. The file is large enough for A4–A3 prints, posters and postcards.

    Can I keep my commission private?

    Sure, just tell me. By default I may share finished pieces in my portfolio and on Instagram.

    Do you draw in other styles?

    I stay in my own style, but I can go softer or brighter, more realistic or more stylised — just show me what you like.

    Is it a gift? Can you send it on a date?

    Yes — mention the date in your request and I’ll plan the queue around it. Book at least 3 weeks ahead to be safe.
